Sample 2026 Cross-Border Tax Filing Update

This sample notice is used to verify the Tax Monitor pipeline. In April 2026, a draft regulation introduces a mandatory filing obligation for multinational groups with cross-border service income.

The effective date is July 1, 2026. Affected companies must review their compliance workflow, update internal tax controls, and prepare supporting documents before the first filing deadline.

Late submission may trigger penalty exposure, audit review, and additional documentation requests from the tax authority. Management should assign an owner, confirm reporting thresholds, and monitor subsequent guidance.
